Heart Health

Everyday Habits That Support Heart Health

Heart health is influenced by many factors, including physical activity, diet, smoking, sleep and blood pressure.

Key points

  • Be physically active
  • Eat a varied, balanced diet
  • Avoid tobacco
  • Know your blood pressure when appropriate
  • Discuss personal risk factors with a health professional

Making healthy choices

Healthy habits work best when they are realistic and sustainable. Your needs can vary because of age, activity, medical history, medicines, pregnancy and other factors. If you have a health condition or are unsure what is right for you, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.

Remember

This article is for general education. It is not a diagnosis or a substitute for individualized medical advice.

HealthNjoro health notice: Seek urgent local medical care for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms. For emergencies, contact your local emergency service.
